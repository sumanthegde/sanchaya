
The shehenshah we see in Persian and Urdu is actually the modern form of क्षायतीयानां क्षायतीयः, king of kings, like a चक्रवर्ती


क्षा = rule
क्षत्र = ruler (same in our Vedic dictionary too)
क्षाय = kingdom or dominion

Xerxes is gross misspelling of 𐎧𐏁𐎹𐎠𐎼𐏁𐎠(Xšayāršā, क्षायार्षः). Made up of क्षायः kingdom + ऋषः bull or hero, very much equivalent to our राजर्षभः

Artaxerxes is 𐎠𐎼𐎫𐎧𐏁𐏂𐎠(अर्ताक्षत्रः or ऋतक्षत्रः) romanised as r̥taxšaθrah

ऋतं = the cosmic rythm, truth
क्षत्रः = ruler

Darius is a misspelling in English. Corruption from the already corrupted form in Greek. 

The actual Persian name written in the inscription is  𐎭𐎠𐎼𐎹𐎺𐎢𐏁 (Dārayavaʰuš). संस्कृतच्छाया is धारयवसुः
